## Highlights

### 1. PIEZO1 GLD variants pharmacologically rescuable in vitro — Yoda1 analog identified
*(iScience, published Aug 2025 — not previously captured)*

A functional electrophysiology study characterized 4 PIEZO1 variants in GLD patients and showed the mechanism is **reduced mechanical channel sensitivity** (not reduced expression). Yoda1 partially restored channel activation; a synthesized Yoda1 analog showed enhanced potency in patch-clamp.

This is the first in vitro demonstration that PIEZO1 GLD variants are pharmacologically rescuable, and identifies PIEZO1 agonist pharmacology as a potential therapeutic strategy for genetically defined GLD.

**Confidence limits:** Entirely in vitro. No lymphedema animal model. Yoda1 has no clinical history for lymphatic disease. Lymphedema has a poor preclinical-to-clinical translation record. Paper is ~10 months old.

> Importance **8**/10 | Evidence **5**/10 | Novelty **5**/10 (stale) | Decision usefulness **4**/10
> hypothesis-generating | full-paper-published | primary lymphedema type

### 2. LVA/VLNT umbrella SR supports new AVF/AVLS guidelines; primary LE explicitly included
*(JVS Venous and Lymphatic Disorders, published 2026-05-27 — new this week)*

26-SR umbrella review: LVA reduces cellulitis from 0.84 → 0.07 episodes/year, limb volume by 34–47%; QoL improved in 50–100% of patients. LYMPHA reduces lymphedema incidence by 30.3 per 100 patients. LVA **explicitly supported for primary lymphedema** when functional channels are present.

**Confidence limits:** Underlying evidence is almost universally single-arm and retrospective. Primary LE is a minority population in source studies (BCRL dominates). Volume is a surrogate. Obesity confounding not addressed. Surgical outcomes caution applies.

> Importance **7**/10 | Evidence **6**/10 | Novelty **9**/10 | Decision usefulness **6**/10
> practice-relevant | full-paper-published | both (primary + secondary LE)

### 3. Primary LE tissue shows reduced fibrosis, impaired endothelial barrier — unexpected pathophysiology
*(Lymphatic Research and Biology, published 2026-05-26 — new this week)*

Monocentric case-control study (n=15 primary LE patients vs n=8 controls): reduced cutaneous fibrosis (opposite of secondary LE), downregulation of CLDN5, VEGFD, and TJP1 in affected limbs. Microsurgical outcomes variable.

**Confidence limits:** Small n; surgical selection bias; no genetic subtyping. Replication required before mechanistic conclusions.

> Importance **6**/10 | Evidence **4**/10 | Novelty **8**/10 | Decision usefulness **3**/10
> hypothesis-generating | full-paper-published | primary

### 4. Prenatal exome/genome sequencing identifies PIEZO1/GLD in 3% of fetal effusion cases
*(Prenatal Diagnosis, Jan 2026)*

303 fetal effusion pregnancies: 8 (3%) had molecular diagnosis of AD lymphedema or GLD. PIEZO1 heterozygous carriers had more favorable perinatal outcomes. Four fetuses inherited variants from parents with milder phenotypes.

> Importance **6**/10 | Evidence **5**/10 | Novelty **4**/10 (stale) | Decision usefulness **5**/10
> hypothesis-generating | full-paper-published | primary
