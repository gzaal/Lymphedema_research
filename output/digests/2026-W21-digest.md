### 3. Maternal sirolimus for prenatal KHE — first experience

**PMID:** 42142389 | **Journal:** Prenatal Diagnosis  
**DOI:** 10.1002/pd.70180

**What happened:** A retrospective review of four prenatal KHE cases reports that maternal sirolimus therapy was associated with early tumor regression on fetal MRI in one case, representing the first reported antenatal use of maternal sirolimus for KHE.

**Why people may care:** Prenatal KHE with Kasabach-Merritt phenomenon carries high morbidity and mortality; maternal sirolimus as an antenatal intervention is a novel hypothesis that could inform rare fetal vascular anomaly management.

### 5. PROX1 adult conditional knockout — lymphatic identity requires ongoing PROX1 expression

**PMID:** 42084939 | **Journal:** JCI Insight  
**DOI:** 10.1172/jci.insight.203711

**What happened:** Conditional adult Prox1 knockout in mouse Schlemm's canal caused rapid, sustained ocular hypertension (24 weeks), with the canal reverting to vein-like phenotype — demonstrating that PROX1 is required to maintain lymphatic-like identity in adult tissue, not only during development.

**Why people may care:** PROX1 is a master transcription factor for lymphatic endothelial identity and mutations are associated with primary lymphedema; understanding PROX1's ongoing adult maintenance role has mechanistic implications for primary lymphedema progression.

**What limits confidence:** Mouse model; the study context is glaucoma/Schlemm's canal, not lymphedema — direct translation to primary lymphedema pathophysiology requires separate investigation.
