## ALERT — Hypothesis-Generating — RAS/MAPK pathway implicated in primary lymphedema in 408-patient cohort

**What happened:** A retrospective NGS analysis of 408 Italian primary lymphedema patients identified five pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants in LZTR1, RAF1, and MAP2K1 (RAS/MAPK pathway), four of which are novel; in silico molecular modeling supports pathogenicity of a RAF1 missense variant (c.1344T>G; p.Ile448Met).

**Why people may care:** RAS/MAPK pathway genes are not currently on standard primary lymphedema diagnostic panels; if validated, these findings could improve genetic diagnostic yield for the substantial proportion of primary lymphedema patients who currently lack a causative variant.

**What limits confidence:** All variant pathogenicity assessments rely on in silico modeling without functional validation; the same 408-patient Italian cohort has been the subject of multiple Lymphology publications by this group analyzing distinct gene sets, raising concern about selective reporting and p-hacking.

**Classification:** hypothesis-generating

**Study design:** Retrospective observational cohort, NGS panel, single center (Italian), n=408
**Publication event:** full-paper-published
**Endpoint:** exploratory (variant identification, in silico pathogenicity)
**Comparator:** none
**Result:** positive
**Skepticism flags:** Retrospective; in silico validation only; narrow Italian cohort; multiple companion papers from same cohort examining different pathways (series effect)

## ALERT — Hypothesis-Generating — Transcription factor network variants (FOXC1/NOTCH1/RORC/FOXC2/SOX18) in 408 primary lymphedema patients

**What happened:** Analysis of the same 408-patient Italian primary lymphedema cohort identified 18 likely pathogenic variants in FOXC1, NOTCH1, RORC, FOXC2, and SOX18 — most novel — and recommends adding FOXC1, NOTCH1, and RORC to standard diagnostic panels alongside the already-established FOXC2 and SOX18.

**Why people may care:** If FOXC1, NOTCH1, and RORC are confirmed as pathogenic in primary lymphedema, this would materially expand the genetic diagnostic landscape and could inform gene-specific management strategies.

**What limits confidence:** In silico validation only, no functional studies; this is one of at least three Lymphology papers by the same group examining different gene sets in overlapping Italian cohorts, making it difficult to assess independence and increasing the risk of false positives from repeated testing.

**Classification:** hypothesis-generating

**Study design:** Retrospective observational cohort, NGS panel, single center (Italian), n=408
**Publication event:** full-paper-published
**Endpoint:** exploratory (variant identification, in silico pathogenicity)
**Comparator:** none
**Result:** positive
**Skepticism flags:** Retrospective; in silico validation only; narrow Italian cohort; series of companion papers from same cohort (FOXC2 and SOX18 were already known — the novel claim here is FOXC1/NOTCH1/RORC)

## Notable Adds — Below Alert Threshold

The following papers were added to papers.json this scan. They did not meet the alert threshold (importance ≥ 7 AND evidence ≥ 5) but represent significant additions to the database.

### LPA4/LPA6 → FOXC2 → Lymphatic Valve Development (JCI, March 2026)
- **PMID:** 41528806 | Importance 7, Evidence 4
- LPA receptors regulate FOXC2 in mouse lymphatic endothelial cells; provides new mechanistic context for FOXC2-related primary lymphedema syndromes. Preclinical only.

### CCLA Infants Treated with mTOR/MEK Inhibitors (Eur J Pediatrics, August 2025)
- **PMID:** 40835771 | Importance 8, Evidence 3
- Case series of 7 neonates with central conducting lymphatic anomaly treated with sirolimus ± trametinib; substantial clinical improvement with no severe adverse events. Uncontrolled, n=7 — cannot be generalized, but clinically informative for a condition with limited options.

### NGS Panel for Vascular Anomalies Achieves 61.5% Diagnostic Yield in LM (Lymphatic Research and Biology, January 2026)
- **PMID:** 41574430 | Importance 6, Evidence 5
- Tissue-based 735-gene somatic NGS panel identified pathogenic variants in 61.5% of lymphatic malformation samples; somatic mosaicism detected to 1% allele fraction. Practical diagnostic tool when germline testing is negative.

### Prenatal FLT4 Variants in Congenital Lymphedema (Frontiers in Genetics, November 2025)
- **PMID:** 41328432 | Importance 5, Evidence 2
- Three fetal cases with FLT4 mutations including one novel variant; adds to prenatal counseling data for Milroy-type lymphedema.

### TIE1-FOXO1-FOXC2 Axis in Lymphatic Valve Development (Development, June 2025)
- **PMID:** 40539480 | Importance 6, Evidence 4
- Mouse study establishing TIE1 as the dominant ANG/TIE receptor in lymphatic endothelial cells, operating through FOXO1 to regulate FOXC2 expression.

### HGF/MET Pathway in Primary Lymphedema (Lymphology, 2024)
- **PMID:** 40245437 | Importance 5, Evidence 3
- 8 HGF/MET/CBL variants in 8 Italian primary lymphedema patients; another single-pathway paper from the Bertelli/Michelini cohort.
