## ALERT — Hypothesis-generating — KRAS-mutant lymphatic malformations lose valves through matrix metalloproteinases, and MMP inhibition rescues valve formation in vitro

**What happened:** An ATVB mechanistic study uses lymphatic-restricted KRAS-G12D mice (Flt4CreERT2;Kras-LSL-G12D;Prox1GFP) and KRAS-G12D-expressing human dermal lymphatic endothelial cells to show that KRAS activation impairs lymphatic valve development by upregulating matrix metalloproteinases, and that MMP inhibition in 3D collagen culture rescues valve formation.

**Why people may care:** Identifies MMPs as a candidate druggable axis downstream of KRAS in lymphatic malformations with chylothorax / chylous ascites, complementing existing trametinib (MEK) strategies for RASopathy-associated lymphatic disease and providing a tractable mechanistic node distinct from MEK.

**What limits confidence:** Mechanistic findings rely on a single mouse model and in-vitro 3D culture rescue rather than in-vivo MMP inhibition; no patient-tissue MMP validation in the abstract; no functional readout for chylothorax or lymphedema in MMP-treated animals; clinical translation requires in-vivo rescue and biomarker development.

**Classification:** hypothesis-generating
**Study design:** preclinical-animal (lymphatic-restricted Cre + Kras-LSL-G12D mouse) plus in-vitro 3D human LEC culture
**Publication event:** full-paper-published
**Endpoint:** surrogate (valve quantification on Prox1GFP reporter; MMP mRNA/protein; in-vitro valve formation)
**Comparator:** active (KRAS-WT vs KRAS-G12D, MMP-inhibitor vs vehicle)
**Result:** positive
**Skepticism flags:** narrow_population (somatic-KRAS-mutant LM subset)
