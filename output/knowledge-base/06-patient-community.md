## Patient Advocacy Organizations

### LE&RN — Lymphatic Education & Research Network

**Website:** lymphaticnetwork.org
**Based in:** United States (international reach)

LE&RN is the most prominent global advocacy and research funding organization for lymphedema and lymphatic diseases. Key activities:

- **LE&RN International Lymphatic Disease Registry & Biorepository** (hosted at Stanford) — the primary biobank and patient registry for lymphedema research in North America
- Funds investigator-initiated research grants
- Annual symposium (see Conferences below)
- Patient education resources in multiple languages
- Advocates for lymphedema recognition and insurance coverage

### National Lymphedema Network (NLN)

**Website:** lymphnet.org
**Based in:** United States

The NLN publishes **position papers** on lymphedema management standards, establishes standards for lymphedema therapist training (CLT certification requirements), and provides patient education. Their position papers on topics like safe exercise and surgical considerations are frequently cited in clinical guidance.

### International Lymphoedema Framework (ILF)

**Website:** lympho.org
**Based in:** United Kingdom (international)

The ILF coordinates national lymphedema frameworks across multiple countries and runs the **LIMPRINT** (Lymphedema IMpact and PRevalence INTernational) international prevalence and outcomes database. It publishes consensus best practice documents and facilitates cross-country comparison of lymphedema care standards.

### Lymphoedema Support Network (LSN)

**Website:** lymphoedema.org
**Based in:** United Kingdom

UK-based patient charity providing education, support groups, and advocacy. Publishes the *LymphLine* newsletter and maintains a therapist directory for UK patients.

### European Group for Lymphology (EGL)

Pan-European scientific and patient network. Facilitates collaboration between national lymphology societies and organizes the European lymphology conference.

## International Scientific Organizations

### ISL — International Society of Lymphology

**Website:** isl.arizona.edu
**Role:** Issues the definitive staging and treatment guidelines (ISL Consensus Document, most recent 2020/2021). Organizes the biennial ISL World Congress (see Conferences). Membership includes leading clinicians and researchers globally.

### Lymphatic Research and Biology Journal

**Publisher:** Mary Ann Liebert (Liebertpub)
**Website:** liebertpub.com/lrb
**Role:** The primary peer-reviewed journal for lymphedema and lymphatic disease research. Published in partnership with LE&RN. Essential reference for staying current with the literature.

### Society for Vascular Surgery (SVS)

Increasingly involved in lymphedema due to overlap with vascular surgery and lymphatic microsurgery. Publishes relevant clinical guidelines.

### American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) / BAPRAS (UK)

Active in developing surgical standards for LVA, VLNT, and LYMPHA procedures.

## Registries and Research Databases

### LIMPRINT (Lymphedema IMpact and PRevalence INTernational)

**Run by:** International Lymphoedema Framework
**Purpose:** International epidemiological database; Phase I (2013–14) and Phase II (2014–17) established prevalence data across 9 countries and 40 sites. National LIMPRINT databases continue in Japan, Canada, Australia, the UK, and others.
**Data collected:** Prevalence, severity (ISL staging), treatment type, quality of life, demographic factors

**Key finding:** Lymphedema affects an estimated 1.33 in 1,000 individuals in the general population; much higher in cancer survivors and specific patient groups.

### LE&RN International Lymphatic Disease Registry & Biorepository

**Hosted at:** Stanford University
**Website:** lernregistry.stanford.edu
**Purpose:** Confidential patient registry linking clinical data with biospecimens (blood, tissue). Designed to support trial design, biomarker discovery, and therapeutic target identification. Enrollment open to patients globally.

### CTG / ClinicalTrials.gov

**Website:** clinicaltrials.gov
Search "lymphedema" to find currently active, recruiting trials. As of April 2026, approximately 150–200 active lymphedema trials are registered globally.

### Orphanet

**Website:** orpha.net
Comprehensive rare disease database with entries for each genetic lymphedema syndrome (Milroy disease, lymphedema-distichiasis, Hennekam syndrome, etc.). Each entry includes OMIM links, genetics, clinical features, diagnostic approaches, and management summaries. Invaluable for patients with primary lymphedema syndromes.

### OMIM (Online Mendelian Inheritance in Man)

**Website:** omim.org
Gene-disease association database. Essential for understanding genetic causes and inheritance patterns of primary lymphedema syndromes.

## Key Conferences

### ISL World Congress

**Frequency:** Every two years
**Organizer:** International Society of Lymphology
**Focus:** Comprehensive — basic science, clinical trials, surgical techniques, guidelines updates, patient care

The ISL World Congress is the premier global scientific meeting in lymphology. Landmark publications, guideline updates, and major trial results are frequently presented here first. Attendance by lymphedema therapists, surgeons, geneticists, and basic scientists.

### LE&RN Annual Symposium

**Frequency:** Annual
**Organizer:** Lymphatic Education & Research Network
**Focus:** Primarily research-oriented; heavily attended by academic clinicians and laboratory scientists in North America

Features presentation of NIH-funded and LE&RN-funded research. Often includes sessions on emerging pharmacological approaches, gene therapy, and novel imaging techniques.

### BRAS (Breast Reconstruction and Awareness) and Oncoplastic Surgery Conferences

Increasingly relevant because BCRL prevention (ILR/LYMPHA) is part of the oncological surgery agenda. ISL and LE&RN are working to integrate lymphedema prevention into mainstream oncological surgery conferences.

### European Congress on Lymphology (EGL)

European conference covering the full scope of lymphatic disease including lymphedema, chylous disorders, and lymphatic anomalies.

### ASPS Annual Meeting (American Society of Plastic Surgeons)

Major venue for LVA, VLNT, and LYMPHA surgical technique updates and trial results.

## Quality of Life Measurement Tools

Lymphedema substantially impairs quality of life. Validated, disease-specific patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) are essential for clinical trials and routine care.

### LYMPH-Q

**Development:** Rigorous methodology using item response theory; developed with extensive patient input; validated in multiple languages

**Version:** The **LYMPH-Q Upper Extremity** module was developed and validated in 2024 (*Journal of Patient-Reported Outcomes*). Covers: symptoms (arm heaviness, tightness), function (activities of daily living, arm use), psychological impact (body image, emotional impact), and satisfaction with treatment.

A **Lower Extremity** module is under development.

**Status:** Being incorporated into clinical trials globally as a standard PROM for BCRL.

### LYMQOL

**Development:** UK-based; older validation; widely used in Europe
**Versions:** LYMQOL Arm and LYMQOL Leg
**Domains:** Function, appearance, symptoms/pain, mood
**Cross-cultural validation:** Ongoing in multiple languages

LYMQOL is currently the most widely used validated lymphedema PROM globally due to its earlier development and broader language availability. It is used in the LIMPRINT studies and many European clinical trials.

### ULL-27

Validated French/European lymphedema QoL questionnaire. Used in French and some Dutch/Belgian centers.

### Disability of the Arm, Shoulder and Hand (DASH) and QuickDASH

Non-lymphedema-specific but frequently used in BCRL trials as a functional outcome measure.

## Netherlands-Specific Resources

### NVFL (Nederlandse Vereniging voor Fysiotherapie bij Lymfologische en Oncologische Aandoeningen)

**Website:** nvfl.nl
The professional organization of lymphedema therapists in the Netherlands. Maintains the national register of certified lymphedema therapists (oedeem/lymfetherapeuten), publishes Dutch-language patient education, and sets training standards.

### Finding a Lymphedema Therapist in the Netherlands

The NVFL website maintains a searchable therapist directory. Look for therapists with "Oedeem Fysiotherapeut" or "Lymfetherapeut" certification. Most major cities have certified practitioners; coverage in rural areas is more limited.

### Dutch Reference Centers for Lymphedema Surgery

**Maastricht UMC+**: Leading program for LVA and VLNT in the Netherlands. Dr. René van der Hulst and colleagues have published extensively on supermicrosurgical lymphedema treatment.

**Erasmus MC Rotterdam**: Major oncological center; active in BCRL prevention and management.

**Amsterdam UMC / AMC**: Research-active in lymphatic biology; clinical program for complex lymphedema.

**LUMC Leiden**: Genetic lymphedema program; clinical genetics expertise for primary lymphedema workup.

**Radboud UMC Nijmegen**: Multidisciplinary oncological and genetic programs.

### Dutch Insurance Coverage

Lymphedema compression garments are covered under basic insurance (basisverzekering) in the Netherlands when prescribed by a physician or certified lymphedema therapist, subject to a maximum insured amount. CDT/MLD sessions are covered under the physiotherapy supplementary insurance (aanvullende verzekering); coverage duration varies by insurer. LVA and VLNT surgery are covered at reference centers when medically indicated. Patients should verify current reimbursement conditions with their insurer (zorgverzekeraar).

### Lymphedema Patient Support in Dutch

- **Lymfnetwerk Nederland**: Patient support network with local groups across the Netherlands
- **NFK (Nederlandse Federatie Kankerpatiëntenorganisaties)**: Umbrella organization for Dutch cancer patient groups including those with BCRL; advocacy for insurance coverage and care standards
- **Borstkankervereniging Nederland (BVN)**: Breast cancer patient organization; active in BCRL prevention advocacy; provides information in Dutch

## Finding Care and Clinical Trials

### For Patients Seeking Specialized Care

1. **Ask your GP (huisarts) for a referral** to a center with a certified lymphedema therapist
2. **Seek surgical consultation** at one of the Dutch reference centers (Maastricht UMC+, Erasmus MC, AMC) if CDT is not sufficient — early surgery gives better results
3. **Consider ICG lymphography** at a specialized center before deciding on surgical approach

### For Patients Interested in Clinical Trials

- **ClinicalTrials.gov**: Search "lymphedema" — filter by Country (Netherlands) or proximity
- **EU Clinical Trials Register (EudraCT)**: euclinicaltrials.eu — European trials specifically
- **LE&RN Registry**: Enrolling at lernregistry.stanford.edu — not a trial but contributes data to future research
- **Ask at your reference center**: Many Dutch centers participate in international trials and may offer access

### Telemedicine and International Consultations

For complex primary lymphedema or surgical consultation, many of the major European and US programs (Maastricht, Erasmus, USC, NYU Langone, MD Anderson) offer telemedicine consultations for initial evaluation before requiring in-person visits.
